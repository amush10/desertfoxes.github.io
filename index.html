🏁 2025: EVGP OVERVIEW 🏁

The 2025 UAE EVGP was held at the Dubai Kartdrome on February 22 and featured 43 international high school teams from the UAE, USA, Italy, Kuwait, and Egypt.

It was organized by Global EEE and 3elm Education & Training Consultancy, in partnership with ProjoTech, PepsiCo, and the U.S. Mission to the UAE.

As a global platform for innovation, sustainability, and youth empowerment, the EVGP fosters international collaboration and showcases the future of green engineering through hands-on STEM excellence.

The event brought together 850+ student participants, marking a record turnout and highlighting the growing momentum for electric vehicle innovation.

Fueling innovation and teamwork, competition empowered students to showcase their creativity and technical skills by designing, building, and racing fully functional electric vehicles.
